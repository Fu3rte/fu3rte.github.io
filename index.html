About

I'm Finnian — 陈祺宏, Qihong Chen — a developer in Dongguan, China. The name is Old Irish for light, spirit and clarity; I keep it because it reads easier than a pinyin nobody spells right.

I spent years making pixels behave — interfaces, motion, the small delights that make a page feel alive. Then I met large language models and never looked back. Now I build AI applications: agents that plan, retrieve and act — still wrapped in interfaces that feel considered.

Away from the screen I train calisthenics, live in music, and take photographs of things before they move.
